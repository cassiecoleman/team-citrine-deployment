## Epic 1: Ride Booking & Scheduling

### US01 — Schedule a Recurring Ride
**As a** single parent,
**I want** to schedule recurring rides for my child on specific days and times,
**so that** I can ensure reliable transportation to school or activities without rebooking every day.

---

### US02 — Book a Ride in Advance
**As a** car-free commuter,
**I want** to book a ride up to 7 days in advance and receive a confirmed pickup time,
**so that** I can plan my work schedule around dependable transportation and never be late.

---

## Epic 2: Safety Features

### US03 — Assign a Trusted Driver for Child Rides
**As a** parent,
**I want** to designate approved drivers and require identity verification before my child's ride begins,
**so that** I can feel confident my child is only picked up by a vetted, trusted driver.

---

### US04 — Share a Live Trip with an Emergency Contact
**As a** parent sending my child on a ride,
**I want** to automatically share a live trip link with a designated emergency contact,
**so that** a trusted adult can monitor the ride in real time without needing the app.

---

## Epic 3: Pricing & Payments

### US05 — Lock In a Predictable Weekly Rate
**As a** budget-conscious rider,
**I want** to subscribe to a weekly ride pass at a fixed price for my regular commute route,
**so that** I can budget my transportation costs without worrying about surge pricing.

---

### US06 — Split a Fare with Another Rider
**As a** budget-conscious commuter,
**I want** to split the cost of a ride with another Ultra user traveling a similar route,
**so that** I can reduce my daily transportation expenses while still getting door-to-door service.

---

## Epic 4: Driver Management

### US07 — Rate and Flag a Driver
**As a** parent,
**I want** to rate my child's driver after each trip and flag any concerns directly to Ultra support,
**so that** the platform maintains high driver quality and I can protect my child's safety over time.

---

## Epic 5: Real-Time Tracking

### US08 — Track My Ride Live on a Map
**As a** car-free commuter,
**I want** to view my driver's real-time location on a map from the moment they accept my ride,
**so that** I know exactly when to step outside and can time my departure from work or home precisely.

---

## Epic 6: Notifications & Alerts

### US09 — Receive Ride Status Alerts via SMS
**As a** parent who may not always have the app open,
**I want** to receive SMS notifications when my child's ride is confirmed, when the driver arrives, and when the trip ends,
**so that** I stay informed throughout the journey without needing to watch the app constantly.

---

## Epic 7: Account Management

### US10 — Manage Multiple Rider Profiles Under One Account
**As a** single parent with more than one child,
**I want** to create separate rider profiles for each of my children under a single account, each with their own photo and emergency contacts,
**so that** I can book and monitor rides for multiple kids without juggling separate logins or losing track of trip history.
