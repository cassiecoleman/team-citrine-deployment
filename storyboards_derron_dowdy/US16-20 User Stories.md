# Ride‑Sharing App User Stories (Metro City Persona)

## US16 – Compare Ride vs Public Transit Time

**As a** person who lives in a big metro like New York,
**I want to** compare ride time with public transit
**So that** I can choose the fastest option.

---

## US17 – Split Fare Instantly

**As a** person who lives in a big metro like New York,
**I want to** instantly split fares with friends
**So that** group outings are easier.

---

## US18 – Request Shared Ride Option

**As a** person who lives in a big metro like New York,
**I want to** request a shared ride option
**So that** I can save money during peak hours.

---

## US19 – Avoid High‑Traffic Routes

**As a** person who lives in a big metro like New York,
**I want to** use optimized routing to avoid heavy traffic
**So that** I arrive faster.

---

## US20 – Book Late‑Night Safe Ride

**As a** person who lives in a big metro like New York,
**I want to** book a verified late‑night driver option
**So that** I feel safe traveling after dark.
