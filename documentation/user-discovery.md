# User Discovery

## Startup Concept

What problem is your product solving?

Some US consumers do not have a car, and both taxis and public transportation are hard to use in many cities in the United States, while Uber and Lyft also have many deficiencies.

What is its solution?

Provide a new entrant in the ride-share taxi hailing segment. Compete with Uber and Lyft on features and price.

What makes the solution special and distinguishes it from the competition?

Subscription option where the user can request a set number of scheduled rides per month. This could be for several use cases, such as parents with chidlren trying to schedule rides to school, workers scheduling their daily commute, seniors with consistent medical appointments and church events etc each month but no car. The pricing model would have kinks, but this could be a good distinguishing feature.

## Semi-Structured Interview Questions

1. How do you currently get around for daily transportation?
2. You are a regular user of taxis in a large city core (NYC, etc). Think of your common frustrations with the process of using a cab.
3. You are a regular user of Uber and/or Lyft. What are your common frustrations with the process of using these apps?
4. In what circumstances do you find yourself hailing a cab as opposed to driving yourself, walking, or using public transportation?
5. How important is price predictability vs. getting the lowest possible fare?
6. How do you feel about scheduling rides in advance vs. requesting on-demand?
7. What safety features matter most to you in a ride-sharing app?
8. How would you feel about being able to favorite/request specific drivers?
9. What would a "commuter package" (e.g., 20 rides/month for a flat fee) be worth to you?
10. If this product existed today, what would stop you from using it?

## Product Name

Though we are unsure about the IP Law implications, we are going with ULTRA, as a play on UBER, since Uber means above, but Ultra means highest. In the real world we would consult a lawyer about this.

## Value Proposition

Ultra offers riders predictable, subscription-based pricing and the ability to book trusted, preferred drivers — turning ride-sharing from an unpredictable expense into a reliable daily service for commuters, parents, and budget-conscious riders.
