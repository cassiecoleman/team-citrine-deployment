# Interview

## 1. Current Transportation Habits

**Interviewer:**

> How do you currently get around for daily transportation?

**Participant:**

> It’s a mix, really. Most days I’m stuck using the big ride-sharing apps because I don’t have a car right now.  
>
> It’s either that or the bus, and honestly the bus is so unreliable. I can’t be late for work, so I end up spending way more than I want on rides just to make sure I actually get there on time.  
>
> It’s super frustrating.

---

## 2. Taxi Usage Frustrations

**Interviewer:**

> You are a regular user of taxis in a large city core (NYC, etc). Think of your common frustrations with the process of using a cab.

**Participant:**

> The biggest thing is the total lack of control. I’ll be standing there waving my arm like a crazy person and three empty cabs just drive past me. It’s insulting.  
>
> When you finally get one, you never know what you're going to get. Sometimes the car smells weird or the driver takes the longest route because they think you don’t know the city.  
>
> The worst part is the price. I’ll take the same trip two days in a row and it costs five dollars more the second time for no reason.

---

## 3. Impact of Price Changes

**Interviewer:**

> How do price shifts affect your daily budget?

**Participant:**

> It’s a nightmare for my bank account. I try to set aside a weekly amount, but then one rainy morning happens and suddenly the price doubles.  
>
> It makes me feel like I’m being punished for needing to get to work. When I see surge pricing I just get angry. It makes it impossible to actually plan my life.

---

## 4. Uber / Lyft App Frustrations

**Interviewer:**

> What are your common frustrations with using Uber or Lyft?

**Participant:**

> The waiting is awful. It says the driver is two minutes away but they just sit there for five minutes or drive the wrong direction.  
>
> Sometimes they cancel at the last second and you’re stuck.  
>
> I also hate the suggested pickup spots. Sometimes the app tells me to walk two blocks to a random corner even when I’m carrying heavy bags.

---

## 5. Rush Hour Issues

**Interviewer:**

> What frustrations do you have getting rides during rush hour?

**Participant:**

> First the app spins forever “searching for drivers.” You’re standing there getting more anxious by the second.  
>
> Then when someone accepts, the price is outrageous.  
>
> I’ve also had drivers accept the ride and just sit there waiting for me to cancel so they can get the fee. It feels dishonest.

---

## 6. When Do You Choose a Cab?

**Interviewer:**

> In what circumstances do you hail a cab instead of driving, walking, or using public transportation?

**Participant:**

> Usually when I’m desperate.  
>
> - If it’s pouring rain and I have nice clothes on  
> - If I’m running late  
> - If it’s late at night and I’m tired  
>
> Sometimes it feels like a luxury, but other times it feels like a necessity just to stay sane.

---

## 7. Price Predictability vs Cheapest Fare

**Interviewer:**

> How important is price predictability compared to getting the lowest fare?

**Participant:**

> Predictability, hands down.  
>
> I’d rather know the ride costs a fixed amount every time than gamble between $15 and $40 when I wake up.  
>
> The peace of mind is worth it.

---

## 8. Scheduled Rides vs On-Demand

**Interviewer:**

> How do you feel about scheduling rides in advance versus requesting on-demand?

**Participant:**

> Scheduling sounds great in theory, but it often fails in practice.  
>
> I’ve scheduled early airport rides and still ended up standing outside wondering if anyone was coming.  
>
> On-demand is stressful too, but at least you know immediately whether you have a ride.

---

## 9. Safety Features

**Interviewer:**

> What safety features matter most to you in a ride-sharing app?

**Participant:**

> Safety is everything.  
>
> Important things include:
>
> - The car matching the plate and photo
> - Being able to share my trip with a friend
> - Drivers being properly vetted
>
> There’s nothing worse than getting a weird vibe from someone when you’re stuck in a moving car.

---

## 10. Favorite Drivers

**Interviewer:**

> How would you feel about being able to favorite or request specific drivers?

**Participant:**

> That would be a total game changer.  
>
> Some drivers are great and I’d absolutely want to ride with them again.  
>
> Every trip currently feels like rolling the dice.

---

## 11. Commuter Package

**Interviewer:**

> What would a commuter package (e.g., 20 rides per month for a flat fee) be worth to you?

**Participant:**

> That sounds amazing.  
>
> If it covered my commute and removed surge pricing stress, I’d probably pay a couple hundred dollars a month.

---

## 12. Priority Pickup

**Interviewer:**

> Would you want priority pickup if you paid for a commuter package?

**Participant:**

> Absolutely.  
>
> If I’m committing to a monthly plan, I’d expect to be near the front of the line when demand is high.

---

## 13. Product Interest & Price

**Interviewer:**

> If Ultra existed today and offered:
>
> - Scheduled rides  
> - Flat pricing  
> - Commuter packages  
> - Favorite drivers  
>
> Would you use it? What would you pay per ride?

**Participant:**

> In a heartbeat.  
>
> I normally pay around $15–$20 for my commute, but it jumps to $35 during surge.  
>
> I’d happily pay $22–$25 if the price was guaranteed every time.
