# Storyboard: Save Trusted Drivers

**User Story:** As a single mom who is trying to get transport of kids, I want to favorite trusted drivers so that I feel more comfortable booking future rides.

**Persona:** Maria, 34, single working mom. Trust and consistency matter — she wants to build a small circle of reliable drivers.
**Scenario:** Maria just completed a great ride with a driver named James who was friendly and careful with her kids. She wants to save him for future rides.

---

## Panel 1: Rate & Favorite

**Context:** Maria's ride has just ended. She and her two kids have arrived safely at soccer practice. The app automatically presents the post-ride rating screen. James was patient, drove carefully, and even helped the kids with their seatbelts.

**Action:** Maria gives James a 5-star rating. Below the star rating, she notices a heart icon labeled "Add to Trusted Drivers." She taps it immediately — she wants to remember this driver.

**System Response:** The heart icon fills in and briefly animates (pulse effect). A small toast message appears: "James added to Trusted Drivers." The rest of the rating screen remains available so she can also leave a tip.

**Emotion:** Gratitude and relief. Maria is glad the app makes it easy to bookmark a great driver. She feels like the app understands what matters to her as a parent.

## Panel 2: Trusted Driver Confirmation

**Context:** After tapping the heart icon, a confirmation card slides up showing James's full profile as a newly added trusted driver. Maria can see his details and personalize the entry.

**Action:** Maria reviews James's profile — his name, rating, vehicle info, and the number of rides they have shared. She taps the personal note field and types "Great with kids" so she can remember why she saved him.

**System Response:** The app displays a detailed trusted driver card with James's photo placeholder, name ("James R."), rating (4.9 stars), total trips (312), vehicle (Toyota Highlander — White), and rides together (3). The personal note is saved automatically. Two buttons appear at the bottom: "View All Trusted Drivers" and "Done."

**Emotion:** Confidence and control. Maria feels empowered knowing she is building a personal circle of vetted drivers. The note feature makes it feel like her own private reference — not just a generic favorite.

---

## Panel 3: Booking with Preferred Driver Toggle

**Context:** A few days later, Maria needs to book a ride to take her kids to their grandmother's house. She opens the app, enters the destination, and reaches the ride options screen.

**Action:** Maria selects "Child-Safe" from the ride options. Below the ride cards, she notices a "Preferred Drivers" toggle. She switches it on, hoping the app will try to match her with James or another driver she has trusted.

**System Response:** The toggle turns on and the app displays the status text "Searching trusted drivers first..." with a small loading spinner. The "Request Ride" button is grayed out while the search is in progress. The app prioritizes matching with drivers from Maria's trusted list before expanding to the general driver pool.

**Emotion:** Hopeful anticipation. Maria appreciates that the app actively tries to connect her with drivers she already knows and trusts. The explicit "searching trusted drivers first" message reassures her that her preferences matter.

**Wireframe:** [wireframe-3.svg](wireframe-3.svg)

---

## Panel 4: Matched with Trusted Driver

**Context:** After a brief search, the app finds an available trusted driver. The ride confirmation screen loads showing that James has accepted the ride request.

**Action:** Maria sees James's profile card with a prominent "Trusted Driver" badge (star icon). She reviews the details — his name, rating, vehicle, and the note "Your trusted driver — 3 rides together." She smiles and taps "Confirm Ride."

**System Response:** The app displays a map with the route, James's driver card featuring the "Trusted Driver" badge, his ETA (5 minutes away), and two action buttons: "Contact James" and "Share Trip." The trusted driver badge is visually distinct, giving Maria instant recognition that this is someone she has vetted.

**Emotion:** Comfort and trust. Maria feels genuinely safe. She knows James, she knows how he drives, and she knows her kids will be comfortable. The "Trusted Driver" badge is a small detail, but it turns an ordinary ride into a reliable, familiar experience.
