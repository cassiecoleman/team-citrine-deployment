# Storyboard: Add Multiple Stops

**User Story:** As a single mom who is trying to get transport of kids, I want to add multiple stops in one trip so that I can handle school, daycare, and errands efficiently.

**Persona:** Maria, 34, single working mom. Mornings are hectic — two kids need to go to two different places, then she needs to get to work.
**Scenario:** Monday morning. Maria needs to drop off her 9-year-old at Lincoln Elementary, then her 6-year-old at Sunshine Daycare, then head to her office downtown.

---

## Panel 1: Morning Rush — Opening the App

**Context:** It's 7:15 AM on a Monday. Maria has both kids fed, dressed, and ready to go. She opens the RideShare app on her phone while gathering backpacks and lunchboxes. She needs a single ride that can make multiple stops — school first, then daycare, then her office.

**Action:** Maria taps the "Where to?" search bar on the home screen. She notices the "+" icon labeled "Add Stop" just below the destination field and taps it to begin entering multiple destinations for her morning route.

**System Response:** The app expands the destination entry area, revealing a multi-stop input form. The first destination field is active and ready for input, with a blinking cursor. A subtle "+" button appears below it, inviting her to add additional stops. The interface shows a numbered stop list format, making it clear that stops will be visited in order.

**Emotion:** Relieved and hopeful. Maria feels a small wave of relief seeing the multi-stop feature — she won't need to book three separate rides or negotiate with a driver. The clean, simple interface makes a chaotic morning feel slightly more manageable.

## Panel 2: Planning the Route — Entering All Three Stops

**Context:** Maria is still at home. She now needs to enter all three destinations in the correct order: school first for her 9-year-old, daycare second for her 6-year-old, then her downtown office. Timing matters — school starts at 7:45 AM and daycare opens at 7:30 AM, so the order is important.

**Action:** Maria enters her stops one by one: Stop 1 is "Lincoln Elementary" (tagged as a drop-off), Stop 2 is "Sunshine Daycare" (tagged as a drop-off), and Stop 3 is "Maria's Office (Downtown)" as her final destination. She reviews the route summary displayed on the map and the estimated time and fare.

**System Response:** The app displays all three stops in a vertical list with connecting dots and lines on the left side, showing the sequential route. Each stop has a small "x" button to remove it if needed. A map view shows the optimized route connecting all points. Below the stop list, a route summary reads: "3 stops — ~35 min — ~$24." A prominent "Confirm Route" button appears at the bottom.

**Emotion:** Confident and in control. Seeing all three stops laid out visually on the map with time and cost estimates gives Maria a sense of control over her hectic morning. She knows exactly what to expect — no surprises, no mental math about separate fares.

## Panel 3: On the Road — Approaching the First Stop

**Context:** Maria confirmed the route and a driver arrived within 4 minutes. She and both kids are now in the car. They're heading north on Maple Avenue, approaching Lincoln Elementary. Her 9-year-old has his backpack ready. The app is tracking progress in real time.

**Action:** Maria glances at her phone and sees the app showing they are approaching Stop 1. She prepares her older child to exit quickly. The app provides a clear view of the current stop and what comes next, so she can mentally prepare for the rest of the trip.

**System Response:** The app displays a "Trip in Progress" screen with the route drawn on the map, showing all three marked stops. A prominent banner reads "Arriving at Stop 1 of 3." Below it, "Lincoln Elementary" is shown in large text. A progress indicator shows three dots — the first one filled (current), the other two empty (upcoming). The remaining stops are listed: "Next: Sunshine Daycare — 10 min" and "Then: Maria's Office — 20 min." A "Contact Driver" button sits at the bottom.

**Emotion:** Calm and reassured. The real-time tracking and clear stop-by-stop progress makes Maria feel like everything is under control. She doesn't need to give the driver directions or worry about being forgotten at a stop — the app handles the routing and keeps everyone informed.

## Panel 4: All Done — Trip Summary and Review

**Context:** Both kids have been dropped off successfully and on time. Maria has arrived at her downtown office at 8:10 AM — five minutes early. The trip went smoothly, with the driver following the app's optimized route. Maria is stepping out of the car and the app transitions to the trip summary screen.

**Action:** Maria reviews the trip summary showing all three stops with timestamps. She sees the total trip duration and fare. She selects a star rating to rate the trip and considers leaving a note thanking the driver for being patient at the drop-off stops.

**System Response:** The app shows a "Trip Complete" screen with a checkmark. A trip summary card displays the full route as a vertical timeline: "Home (7:20 AM) -> Lincoln Elementary (7:35 AM) -> Sunshine Daycare (7:50 AM) -> Maria's Office (8:10 AM)." The total is shown as "35 min — $23.50." A row of five stars allows Maria to rate the trip. Two buttons appear at the bottom: "Rate Trip" and "Done."

**Emotion:** Grateful and satisfied. Maria feels a genuine sense of accomplishment — all three stops handled in one seamless trip, both kids dropped off on time, and she arrived at work early. The clear summary with timestamps validates that everything went as planned. She's already thinking about using multi-stop for tomorrow's morning routine.
